# VK Cover — Ветеринарная клиника 01

## Goal
Create a VK community cover that feels like a continuation of the user's existing portfolio: strong editorial composition, large readable typography, one dominant visual subject, controlled secondary copy, no template-like icon grids, no generic feature cards.

## Composition
- Format: 1920×768.
- One dominant headline block, preferably 2 lines.
- Visual subject occupies the opposite side and partially enters the typography zone.
- Strong asymmetry; avoid centered corporate-template composition.
- Keep clear negative space around the headline.
- Use depth through photographic foreground/background separation, not decorative clutter.

## Typography
- Main headline: «ВЕТЕРИНАРНАЯ КЛИНИКА».
- Condensed / editorial display sans, bold, high contrast.
- Main headline is the primary visual element.
- Secondary line: «Забота о здоровье ваших питомцев» — small and subordinate.
- Optional location line: «Краснодар» only if needed.
- No feature lists, no multiple badges, no unnecessary icons.

## Visual Direction
- Premium veterinary/medical editorial aesthetic.
- Realistic dog and cat photography.
- Clean clinical environment blended with atmospheric photographic depth.
- Palette: deep navy / cool blue / warm off-white with one restrained accent.
- Lighting: cinematic but natural, soft rim light on animals.
- Background should support text readability rather than compete with it.

## Quality Rules
- No generic stock-banner look.
- No excessive UI cards or pills.
- No tiny text.
- No invented services or claims.
- Readable from a distance.
- The composition must look intentionally art-directed rather than assembled from template blocks.
